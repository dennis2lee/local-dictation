1. Install a speech model

Open Terminal and run one of these. large-v3-turbo is the recommended starting point on a CPU: 1.5 GB, and several times faster than the full model.

"/Applications/Local Dictation.app/Contents/Resources/server/scripts/fetch-model.sh" \
  large-v3-turbo --dest ~/Library/Application\ Support/LocalDictation/models

For the most accurate model instead, use large-v3 (2.9 GB).

2. Allow Local Dictation to type for you

Open System Settings › Privacy & Security › Accessibility and turn on Local Dictation. Without it the shortcut cannot be registered and text cannot be written at your cursor.

3. Check everything is ready

"/Applications/Local Dictation.app/Contents/MacOS/local-dictation" --check

It prints one line per prerequisite and names whatever is missing.

4. Dictate

Open Local Dictation, put your cursor where you want the text, and press Control-Shift-M. Press it again to stop.
